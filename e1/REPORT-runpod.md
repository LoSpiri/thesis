## Notes / caveats

- All runs on Apple MPS (float32, `use_float64=false`, `slerp_precision=float32`).

- **T=1.0 with default decoders is not a meaningful quality regime**: masked/uniform (MDLM/Duo) ancestral sampling at T=1 is too stochastic (gibberish), and S-FLM exact-velocity plateaus at ~12% per the paper. The T=0.1 sweep is the meaningful quality comparison (matches the paper's Fig. 3).

- Sigma-delta threshold `tau = mean|v_0|/K` per layer (N-MDLM adaptive step); K=1 is the headline aggressive-sparsity number.

- Top-1 velocity (WTA) readout variants are stored separately; see `quality_*_topk1_*.json`.
